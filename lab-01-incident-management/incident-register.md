# Lab 01: Incident Register

## Purpose

This file tracks the incidents created during my ServiceNow
incident management practice.

- Incident number: INC0010001
- Short description: User unable to connect to office Wi-Fi
- Urgency:  2 - Medium
- Initial state: New
- Current state: In Progress
- Customer communication: Initial acknowledgement and investigation update

### Investigation notes

- Investigation performed: Ticket creation and status update only.
- Technical diagnosis: Not yet performed.
- Resolution: Not yet resolved.

#### Learning outcome
Practiced creating an incident, recording its number,
changing its state, and communicating with the fictional user.
